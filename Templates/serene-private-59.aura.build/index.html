The island

An island at the end of the map.

Forty-seven acres of palm and white sand, ringed by a private reef. A single resort of twelve overwater villas, reached only by seaplane. Here the day has no schedule but the tide.

The arrivalAn hour from the world,
and then gone from it.

A single seaplane crosses the lagoon at golden hour. From the float to your deck is forty steps. There is no road, no lobby, no one waiting but the sea.

The villas

A room that opens
to the sea

Each villa stands alone over the lagoon, glass walls folding away to a private deck and steps straight into the water. Natural wood, stone and linen, cooled by the trade wind.
